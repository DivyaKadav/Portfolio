MY APPROACH

More than replacing words.

Translation requires understanding what a text means, why it was written, who will read it and how that meaning should function in another language.

01

Accuracy

Preserving the intended meaning of the source text without unnecessary distortion.

02

Context

Interpreting language according to its communicative, linguistic and situational context.

03

Terminology

Maintaining appropriate and consistent terminology across specialized or recurring content.

04

Cultural Adaptation

Recognizing when direct equivalence is insufficient and cultural adaptation is required.
