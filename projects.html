02 LINGUISTIC RESEARCH

Phonological and Morphological Analysis of Ujoni Assamese

A linguistic research project examining phonological and morphological patterns in Ujoni Assamese through primary language data.

The project involved field-based language documentation, collection and organisation of speech data, IPA transcription and linguistic analysis.

The research examined language data across lexical and grammatical categories while documenting phonological and morphological characteristics of the variety.

03 MACHINE TRANSLATION

Comparative Analysis of Human vs Machine Translation

A comparative study of human translation and machine translation across three distinct text types.

The study compares human translation with Google Translate, examining how each handles meaning, linguistic structure, style and culturally dependent information.

Particular attention is given to areas where machine translation may encounter difficulties, including untranslatability, cultural nuance, contextual meaning and stylistic choices.
